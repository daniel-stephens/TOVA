Content note. The fictional emails discuss organizational misconduct, data manipulation, and gene-therapy safety issues.

Your participation in this research is completely voluntary. You may choose not to take part at all. If you decide to participate in this research, you may stop participating at any time. If you decide not to participate in this study or if you stop participating at any time, you will not be penalized or lose any benefits to which you otherwise qualify. For this study, you will explore approximately 600 synthetic internal emails from a fictional biotechnology organization using one assigned interface. Some interfaces use large language models (LLMs) to generate topics or labels. AI-generated information may be inaccurate or incomplete. During a fixed 30-minute exploration period, you will browse emails and work with the assigned tool. You will be told the general kinds of questions that will follow, but not the exact questions. When exploration ends, the tool will close and you will answer four true/false/not-sure questions and four written questions from memory. You may also select the email IDs that support your written answers. You will not be able to reopen the emails during the questionnaire.

You may participate independently or in a live remote one-on-one session. In a moderated session, you will share only the study-tool window, think aloud, and answer neutral questions about how you use and learn from the tool. The researcher will take written notes. No audio, video, screenshots, or screen recording will occur. The full study will take approximately 30 to 60 minutes. Payment and any bonus terms will be stated in the approved recruitment posting before you begin.

Research records may include your questionnaire responses; selected supporting email IDs; themes, topics, or labels displayed or edited during the session; AI interactions where applicable; application logs such as time, emails opened, queries, retrieval results, and topic or label edits; and moderated-session notes where applicable. Data will be linked only to a study-specific code. A recruitment platform may display account information for scheduling and payment, but it will not be copied into the analytic dataset. Please do not enter or say identifying information. The fictional emails discuss organizational misconduct, data manipulation, and gene-therapy safety issues. You may skip a question, stop screen sharing, or stop participation at any time. There are no direct benefits. De-identified responses and AI interactions may be included in research publications or released research datasets.

The research team will use coded responses, selected evidence, themes or labels, applicable AI interactions, application logs, and moderated-session notes to compare factual learning, cross-document reasoning, evidence use, and interaction patterns across the assigned interfaces. Data will be reviewed and anonymized before any public release, and results will be reported without direct identifiers.
